FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is it worth choosing a circular saw over a band saw?

Circular saws typically enable faster, more precise cutting for smaller-to-medium cross-sections, while band saws are advantageous for greater material thickness and longer, quieter cuts. We provide a precise recommendation based on the material, cross-section and quantity.

What is the advantage of the VELOCEX series?

According to manufacturer data, the cutting performance of the VELOCEX high-speed automatic circular saws can be up to twice that of a comparable band saw, with automatic material recognition and speed adjustment.
