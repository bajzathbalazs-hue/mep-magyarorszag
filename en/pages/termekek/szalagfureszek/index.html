MEP band saws

Industrial band saws for metalworking cutting

MEP's band saws are suitable for cutting steel, stainless steel, aluminium and other metals, from small-batch production to high-volume, automated manufacturing. The machine family is available at three levels of automation: manual, semi-automatic and automatic — choose based on your production needs and quantity.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What materials can be cut with a MEP band saw?

MEP's band saws are primarily suitable for cutting steel, stainless steel and aluminium, depending on the machine type and blade fitted. We provide precise material and size limits per model once the final technical data sheets have been uploaded.

What is the difference between manual, semi-automatic and automatic machines?

On a manual machine, the operator starts and supervises the entire cutting cycle; on a semi-automatic machine, the cut is automated but the material clamping is manual; on an automatic machine, both the material feed and the cutting cycle are automated, for high-volume series production.

Can I get professional advice on choosing the right machine type?

Yes — describe your production needs using the form on the contact page, and our colleagues will help you choose the right MEP machine.
