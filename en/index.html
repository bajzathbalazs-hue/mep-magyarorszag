MEP — since 1964

Decades of sawing technology

MEP was founded in 1964 by Enzo Magnani in Pergola, Italy. Today, the MEP Group is one of the leading international players in metalworking saw manufacturing, with four production plants (Italy, Canada, USA, China), 280 employees and around 4,000 machines produced per year — shipping to 65 countries.
