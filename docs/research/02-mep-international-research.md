## 4. Company History (from `/en/the-company/our-history`)

- **Founded 1964** in Pergola (Pesaro-Urbino province, Marche region, Italy) by **Enzo Magnani**. "MEP" = "Magnani Enzo Pergola."
- Enzo Magnani began as a mechanic, gaining skills working with British/American forces in Italy during WWII.
- First product: **TV 300**, a fast abrasive circular sawing machine, built in his small workshop.
- **1970s:** Company became MEP SpA; introduced semi-automatic models (e.g. TRL 300 P).
- **1980s:** Developed mechanical/electronic technology; first automatic machines with automatic bar progression.
- **1990s:** Introduced band sawing machines, expanding the range.
- **2000s:** Acquired by Aksia Group SGR (private equity), forming MEP Group; expansion from local to global player.
- **2007:** Acquired Canadian company **HYDMECH**.
- **2008:** Established **MEP SUZHOU** (China, near Shanghai).
- **2012:** **MEP DO BRASIL** joined the group (South America).
- **2019:** MEP Group became one of five divisions within **Groupe Legris Industries**.
- Current production facilities: **Italy, Canada, USA, China**; distribution to 60+ countries (one source phrased as "over 50 countries").
